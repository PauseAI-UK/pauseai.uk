# New onboarding

Global is rolling out a new join form. We integrate with it, and make
sure it does what we need. Loosely, that's:

- New UK members reach us, as CiviCRM's members do now.
- It asks whether PauseAI UK can email them, text them and call them,
  naming PauseAI UK, so each is a yes we can act on.
- It asks whether they want to volunteer, or says where we learn it,
  so the `volunteering` consent has a source.
- It asks for a postcode, for events near them and their MP.
- What it collects is recorded in `docs/data-protection.md`, with what
  it does to the privacy policy and to erasure.
